
# WEEKLY BUSINESS OPERATIONS REVIEW
## AI TASK DELEGATION

### 1. OBJECTIVE

Analyze the company's weekly commercial pipeline and produce an evidence-based business review.

The goal is to identify priority opportunities, stalled negotiations, operational bottlenecks, and actions that can improve commercial performance.

### 2. CONTEXT

The business operates in the AI solutions and automation market.

The commercial operation requires continuous prospecting, customer follow-up, opportunity management, proposal preparation, and sales conversion.

Management needs a clear view of the pipeline to prioritize activities and allocate time effectively.

### 3. RESOURCES

The AI agent will receive an anonymized dataset containing:

- Opportunity ID
- Business segment
- Opportunity status
- Date of last interaction
- Days since last interaction
- Estimated deal value, when available
- Next planned action
- Current commercial stage
- Relevant notes

The dataset must not contain customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, or confidential business information.

### 4. TASKS

1. Review and organize the dataset.
2. Identify incomplete or inconsistent records.
3. Categorize opportunities by commercial status and commercial stage separately.
4. Identify opportunities without recent follow-up.
5. Highlight opportunities with documented buying signals or clearly defined next steps.
6. Identify commercial bottlenecks.
7. Recommend priorities for the next seven days.
8. Create a measurable action plan.
9. Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, and hypotheses.
10. Never estimate conversion probability without reliable supporting historical data.

### 5. EXECUTION PLAN REQUIRED

Before starting the analysis, present:

- Your understanding of the objective.
- The data you will analyze.
- The methodology you intend to use.
- The criteria for prioritization.
- Potential limitations.
- The expected deliverables.

Wait for human approval before proceeding.

### 6. AUTONOMY LIMITS

You are authorized to analyze the supplied data and prepare recommendations.

You are NOT authorized to:

- Contact customers.
- Send emails or messages.
- Change CRM records.
- Modify financial records.
- Approve commercial proposals.
- Make commitments on behalf of the company.
- Invent missing information.

All external actions require explicit human approval.

### 7. SUCCESS CRITERIA

The task will be considered successful when:

- All conclusions are traceable to the supplied data.
- Missing information is clearly identified.
- Priorities have a transparent justification.
- Recommendations are practical and measurable.
- A seven-day action plan is delivered.
- No unauthorized action is performed.

### 8. DELIVERABLES

1. Executive Business Review.
2. Pipeline Analysis.
3. Priority Opportunities.
4. Risk and Bottleneck Assessment.
5. Seven-Day Action Plan.
6. Human Validation Checklist.

### 9. FINAL INSTRUCTION

Start by presenting the execution plan only.

Do not perform the analysis until the human supervisor approves the plan.

Do not confuse opportunity status with commercial stage.

Do not interpret inactivity as proof of lost business.

Do not estimate conversion probability without reliable supporting historical data.
